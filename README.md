# Synthia AI Child Safety Standards

Synthia AI is committed to providing a safe environment for all users.

## Child Safety Policy

- We prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).
- Users can report harmful content through in-app reporting tools.
- Reported content is reviewed and removed when necessary.
- Accounts violating child safety policies may be suspended or permanently banned.
- We cooperate with relevant law enforcement authorities when required by law.
